## What it is

A lean strategy method: a long-term "True North" is broken down into objectives, reviewed in regular Plan-Do-Check-Act cycles, so daily work stays aligned with the direction.

## How WorkPilot applies it

- Your **North Star** is the True North.
- **Milestones** are the steps on the path.
- Todos and time blocks can link to a milestone, so you can see how much of your week served the North Star ("time aligned").

## What we adapted

Simplified for one person: one North Star and milestones, no X-matrix, no team "catchball". Links are optional.
