## What it is

A tree that links a desired outcome to opportunities, possible solutions and the experiments that test them.

## How WorkPilot applies it

Each opportunity links to a milestone (the outcome) and is tested with a small experiment with a check-in date. Then you adopt, park or drop it.

## What we adapted

"OST-lite": no full tree, one experiment at a time per opportunity.
