# 0008. Opportunities: OST-lite + ICE

- Status: Accepted
- Date: 2026-09-27

## Decision

Opportunities come from the AI or from Capture. Each gets an ICE score (Impact, Confidence, Ease, 1-10), is linked to a milestone, and is tested with a small experiment with a check-in date. Outcome: adopt, park or drop.

## Why

Combines Opportunity Solution Tree (Teresa Torres) and ICE scoring. RICE was rejected: 'Reach' fits product teams, not a single person.
