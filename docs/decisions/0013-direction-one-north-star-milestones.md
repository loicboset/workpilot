# 0013. Direction: one North Star + milestones

- Status: Accepted; one North Star per space since [0031](0031-spaces.md)
- Date: 2026-09-27

## Decision

One North Star with milestones. Todos and time blocks can optionally link to a milestone. Progress = milestones reached. 'Time aligned this week' = share of done todos/blocks linked to a milestone (computed). Opportunities and learnings can link to milestones too.

## Why

Adapted from Hoshin Kanri (True North + milestones). Links are optional to keep the app calm.
