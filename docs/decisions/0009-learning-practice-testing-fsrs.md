# 0009. Learning: practice testing + FSRS

- Status: Accepted
- Date: 2026-09-27

## Decision

The AI turns sources (GitHub, journal, reviews, references) into questions (flashcards, short quizzes), not summaries. Reviews are scheduled with FSRS spaced repetition; topics are interleaved.

## Why

Dunlosky et al. (2013) rate practice testing and distributed practice as high utility. FSRS is open source and Anki's recommended scheduler.
